# Stage 1 Product Specification (Launch Ready)

## Long-term Vision
AI Sales Agent that finds local businesses, understands needs, creates personalized outreach, follows up, and brings interested leads.

## Stage 1 Positioning
**Local Business Opportunity Finder + Safe Outreach Assistant**

Find local businesses that need websites or online booking systems and help freelancers & small agencies reach them safely.

## Target Users
- Freelance web designers & developers
- Small digital agencies (1-10 people)

## Core Opportunity Signals (Stage 1 only)
1. No website or very weak website
2. High reviews + No detectable online booking system

## Must Have Features
- Natural Language Campaign Builder
- Business Discovery (OSM seed + optional Google Places)
- Deterministic Website Checks
- Rule-based Opportunity Score
- Confidence States: Verified / Likely / Unknown / Not Found
- Template + Local LLM Personalization (Ollama)
- Mandatory Human Approval
- ESP-based Sending
- Suppression List + Unsubscribe
- Simple CRM Pipeline
- Hard volume caps

## Out of Scope (Stage 1)
- Automatic sending
- WhatsApp
- Advanced reply intelligence
- Agency/White-label
- Complex multi-service detection
- Proposal generation
